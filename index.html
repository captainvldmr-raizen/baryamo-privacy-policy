BaryaMo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

BaryaMo is an offline personal finance application that helps users manage income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, allowances, bills, reminders, and wallets.

2. Information and Storage

BaryaMo is designed to store financial records locally on the user's device. The app does not require an account or user authentication.

3. Data Sharing

BaryaMo's own features do not intentionally transmit financial records to cloud servers or share them with third parties. Any third-party components included in the app must be considered separately.

4. Offline Operation

The core financial management features are designed to work offline.

5. Data Security and Deletion

Financial records are stored locally on the device. Users should protect their devices with an appropriate screen lock. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may delete locally stored records.

6. Children's Privacy

BaryaMo does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own features.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the BaryaMo Google Play listing.
